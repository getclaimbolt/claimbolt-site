The process

From denial letter
to appeal in 48 hours.

Insurance companies count on you giving up. Most people do — 60% of denied claims are never appealed. We turn your denial into a professional, policy-specific appeal that speaks the language insurers actually respond to.

Tell us about your situation

Fill in the form with your name, email, and a brief description of your denied claim or surprise bill. No documents needed yet — just a few sentences about what happened.

Send us your documents

We'll email you within a few hours with instructions to send your denial letter, Explanation of Benefits (EOB), or itemized bill. A phone photo is perfectly fine — PDF, JPG, or PNG all work.

We find the leverage

Our team reads your denial reason codes, cross-references your plan's specific coverage language, and identifies the exact policy provisions that apply. Most denials have at least one technical error or applicable regulation the insurer didn't account for.

You get a ready-to-send appeal

Within 48 hours of receiving your documents, you get a complete, professionally written appeal letter — with the right citations, the right tone for your specific insurer, and clear instructions for where to send it.
